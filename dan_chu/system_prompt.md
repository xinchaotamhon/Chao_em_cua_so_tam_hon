## What this project is

A Vietnamese eye-care reminder for Windows: it counts screen time, and every 20 minutes it takes the
screen for 20 seconds and asks the person to look away.

## What it adds to the master law

- **The interruption is the product.** A reminder that can be missed has no value, so the screen is
  taken rather than a notification shown; the tray, the timer and the wording all serve that second.
- **The person is a stranger with a mouse.** Anyone may install it, so every screen and every tray entry
  is Vietnamese, says what to do next, and needs no account, no service and no network.
- **The wording is the owner's.** The text is a first draft an AI wrote; the owner decides what the app
  says, and that decision is not delegated to a later session.
